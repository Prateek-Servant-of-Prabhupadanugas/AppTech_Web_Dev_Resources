## 🛠️ Individual Cheat Sheet Blueprint Design

Every single cheat sheet asset is strictly optimized for layout clarity and contains:
1.  **The 5-Minute Refresher:** Bulleted technical definitions of core concepts.
2.  **The Interview Survival Kit:** The top 10 toughest interview questions asked by tech companies (e.g., Google, Amazon, Zomato) regarding that specific topic, complete with optimal technical answers.
3.  **The Snippet Sandbox:** Pasteable, highly optimized boilerplates ready to drop into production apps to resolve layout or architectural friction immediately.
